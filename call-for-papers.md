## Scope and Topics of Interest
The SHAMAN workshop invites original research contributions addressing dependability, safety, security, and assurance challenges in Software-Defined Vehicles (SDVs). Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

- Dependable and secure architectures and design principles;
- Mixed-criticality systems and application isolation;
- Fault tolerance, resilience, and failure management;
- Real-time scheduling, timing analysis, and resource management;
- Deterministic networking and in-vehicle communication;
- Intrusion detection, anomaly detection, and mitigation;
- Trustworthy AI and AI-driven adaptation for resilient vehicles;
- Verification, validation, and runtime assurance;
- Failure-aware orchestration and lifecycle management;
- Human–computer interaction, human factors, and trust in autonomous vehicle functions;
- Dependability assessment, testing, and validation of SDVs;
- Industrial case studies, tools, and experimental evaluations for SDV assurance.

We solicit the following types of contributions:

- **Full papers** presenting original research results.
- **Short papers** presenting work in progress, position papers, or tool/prototype descriptions.
- **Industrial experience reports** describing practical challenges and lessons learned in the SDV domain.

Submissions must be original, unpublished work that is not under review elsewhere, written in English.

## Review Process

All submitted papers will be evaluated through a single-blind peer-review process. Each paper will be reviewed by **at least three** members of the international Technical Program Committee, composed of
experts in the automotive and dependability domains. Papers will be evaluated on originality, relevance to the workshop,
technical soundness, and clarity of presentation.

## Publication

Accepted papers will be published in the **EDCC 2027 conference supplemental proceedings**. At least one author of each
accepted paper is expected to register for the workshop and present the paper in person.
